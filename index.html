The register — editing

Edits here commit straight to data.json on main, which on this repository is the deploy. Every save is previewed first, names what it changed, and is one click from being reverted. Fields you edit become yours: the refresh job may propose a change to them and will never make one.

Editor's journal — name changes

Every entry whose ledger name differs from the register data, and the source of the change. Your corrections apply immediately on this device and export for the weekly job. Rows carry a bronze ° where a name or description has been changed. Descriptions are edited inside the entry — open its row.

✓ A green check means that entry's purchase page was read on the date shown — not inferred from how the brand distributes. Confidence is stated rather than implied: high is a brand source or several credible outlets agreeing, medium is one credible source, low is a single aggregator or an unresolved conflict.

Photographs are drawn from the publication that reported each release and are credited individually. If you hold rights to an image here and want it removed, write to wdi-takedown@conn.llc and it will be taken down.
